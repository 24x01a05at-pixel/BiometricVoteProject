Election Progression Phases

Phase 1
Voter Enrollment

Citizens register online, capturing 3 different profiles (Straight, Left, Right) to build a unique biometric signature map.

Phase 2
Nominations Approval

Candidates file applications along with their party logos. Nominations are reviewed and approved by the Administrator.

Phase 3
Biometric Verification

The Voting Booth opens. System checks liveness (rejects printouts/glare screens) and cross-checks face encodings.

Phase 4
Ballot Casting

Voters anonymously choose their candidates on a cryptographically isolated digital ballot, immediately updating the secure logs.

Phase 5
Certified Tallies

Voting period closes. Results are certified immediately, tie-breaker events resolved, and winner receipts generated.
